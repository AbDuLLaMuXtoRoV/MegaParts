Supplier management

Update contacts, verify sellers and upload XLSX offer lists. Unmatched rows remain in review.

Price-list columns: supplier_sku (required), price, stock, warehouse, brand, reference, and the optional offer labels origin (original / OE / analog / budget), condition (new / used / rebuilt), warranty (months), returns (days) and lead time (days; 0 = in stock now). Uzbek and Russian headers work too (turi, holati, kafolat, qaytarish, muddat). Price lists older than 7 days are shown as possibly outdated and are hidden after 14 days.

Add a seller

Then upload their price list on their card below. Any Excel layout works (.xlsx or .xls): the part names, part numbers, cars, brands, prices, warranty headings and "new arrival" / "sale" marks are read automatically, and new parts join the catalog.
